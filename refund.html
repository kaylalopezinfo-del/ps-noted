Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2026

The short version: If you're not satisfied with P.S. Noted Pro within 7 days of purchase, email us and we'll refund you in full.

7-Day Refund Window

We stand behind P.S. Noted Pro. If you purchase a Pro license and are not completely satisfied for any reason, you may request a full refund within 7 days of your purchase date.

What's covered

  • Full refund within 7 days of purchase
  • Partial refund requests after 7 days considered case by case
  • Technical issues we cannot resolve within a reasonable time
  • Accidental duplicate purchases

What's not covered

  • Refund requests made more than 7 days after purchase without extenuating circumstances
  • Requests citing features clearly described as not included in the plan purchased
  • Chargebacks filed without first contacting us — please reach out, we'll make it right

How to request a refund

Email us at hello@psnoted.com with:

  • The email address used for purchase
  • Your order or transaction ID (optional but helpful)
  • A brief note about what went wrong (optional — we use this to improve)

We process refunds within 2 business days. Funds typically appear in your account within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback with your payment provider without first contacting us, we reserve the right to suspend or terminate your license pending resolution. We encourage you to reach out to us directly at hello@psnoted.com to resolve any payment disputes before involving third parties, as this allows us to address your concerns more efficiently.

License deactivation

Upon processing a refund, your Pro license key will be deactivated and the extension will revert to the Free plan. Your saved data is not deleted — it remains on your device.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@psnoted.com — we typically reply within 24 hours.

Need help instead?

Most issues can be resolved quickly. Before requesting a refund, try our Help Center or email support — we'd love to help you get the most out of P.S. Noted.
