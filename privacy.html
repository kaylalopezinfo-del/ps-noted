Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Effective date: June 2026

P.S. Noted is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. All data you create within the extension is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage APIs.

1. Who We Are

P.S. Noted is a Chrome browser extension developed and operated by an independent developer. Contact us at hello@psnoted.com. Our website is https://psnoted.com.

2. Data We Collect and How We Use It

P.S. Noted stores the following data locally on your device only, using Chrome's storage APIs:

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identifiable information (name, address, phone number)
  • Browsing history or web activity
  • AI conversation content beyond what you explicitly select and save
  • Usage analytics or telemetry data
  • Crash reports or error logs
  • Location data
  • Financial or payment card information (handled entirely by Stripe)

4. Data Storage and Handling

All extension data is stored using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage API:

  • chrome.storage.sync — syncs your saves, profiles, boxes, and triggers across your authorized Chrome devices via your Google account. This sync is managed entirely by Google Chrome.
  • chrome.storage.local — stores device-specific preferences and license information on your local device only.

We do not operate any database or server-side storage of your extension content. Your saved highlights, prompts, profiles, and boxes never leave your device except through Chrome's own sync mechanism.

5. Pro License and Payment Data

If you purchase P.S. Noted Pro:

  • Payment is processed by Stripe. We never receive or store your payment card details.
  • Upon purchase, Stripe provides us with your email address solely to deliver your license key. This email is stored in our license system only for license lookup and delivery.
  • Your email is never used for marketing, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • A randomly generated anonymous device ID is stored locally and sent to our license verification API to enforce the 3-device limit. This ID contains no personally identifiable information.

6. Host Permissions and Website Access

P.S. Noted requests access to websites to inject the sidebar into AI platforms (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) and any sites the user manually enables. The extension:

  • Only reads text that the user explicitly selects and saves
  • Does not read, monitor, or record any other page content
  • Does not transmit any page content to our servers
  • Does not track which websites you visit

7. Third-Party Services

We do not sell or share your data. The only third-party services involved are:

  • Google Chrome Sync — syncs storage across your devices. Governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Stripe — payment processing. Only your email is shared with us post-purchase for license delivery.
  • Resend — transactional email for license key delivery only. No marketing emails.
  • Cloudflare — hosts our license verification API. Only license keys and anonymous device IDs are processed. No personal content data passes through Cloudflare.

8. Data Retention

  • Extension data — retained on your device until you clear it via Settings → Clear all data, or uninstall the extension.
  • License key and email (Pro users) — retained in our license system for as long as you hold an active license. Request deletion at any time by emailing hello@psnoted.com.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data by emailing hello@psnoted.com
  • Clear all locally stored extension data at any time via Settings ⚙ → Clear all data
  • Uninstall the extension at any time, which removes all locally stored data

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under GDPR including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

10. Children's Privacy

P.S. Noted is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when we do. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
