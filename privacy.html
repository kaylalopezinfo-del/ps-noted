Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

The short version: P.S. Noted stores all your data locally on your device using Chrome's built-in sync storage. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on external servers. We have no servers. We see nothing.

1. What data we store

P.S. Noted stores the following data exclusively on your device using Chrome's chrome.storage.sync and chrome.storage.local APIs:

  • Highlights and prompts you save from AI conversations
  • Profile names, colors, and emoji you create
  • Boxes and collections you organize
  • Keyword triggers you define
  • Your sidebar position preference (left or right)
  • A randomly generated device ID for device limit enforcement

All of this data lives in your browser's local and sync storage. It is never sent to any server operated by us or any third party.

2. Chrome Sync

If you are signed into Chrome with a Google account and have Chrome Sync enabled, Chrome may sync your extension storage across your devices. This sync is handled entirely by Google's infrastructure — we have no access to this data in transit or at rest. This is the same sync mechanism used by Chrome's built-in bookmarks and settings.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers
  • We do not track which AI platforms you use
  • We do not read, store, or transmit the content of your AI conversations
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts
  • We do not sell data to anyone — there is no data to sell

4. Permissions we request

P.S. Noted requests the following browser permissions:

  • storage — to save your highlights, prompts, profiles, and preferences locally
  • activeTab — to inject the sidebar into the current tab when you open it
  • scripting — to allow the popup to open the sidebar on the active page
  • host permissions (optional) — to enable P.S. Noted on sites beyond the default AI platforms, only when you explicitly grant this per-site

We do not request permissions to read your browsing history, access your clipboard without interaction, or communicate with external servers.

5. Pro plan and payments

P.S. Noted Pro is a one-time purchase processed through Gumroad. Payment processing is handled entirely by Gumroad — we do not receive or store your payment card details. Please refer to Gumroad's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle payment data.

After purchase, your Pro license is verified locally. No account creation is required.

6. Children's privacy

P.S. Noted is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how P.S. Noted handles your data, please reach out at hello@psnoted.com.
